NOTIFICATION SOUNDS — REQUIRED WAV FILES
=========================================

Place the following WAV files in this folder:

  notify_default.wav    — General notification ping (iOS-style or neutral)
  notify_harvest.wav    — Positive chime for harvest-ready
  notify_urgent.wav     — Alert sound for overdue harvest / storm
  notify_done.wav       — Soft completion sound for worker done
  notify_alert.wav      — Warning sound for worker stuck / fuel low
  notify_info.wav       — Neutral info sound for silo full
  notify_weather.wav    — Ambient/weather notification sound

Specs:
  Format:     WAV (PCM, 16-bit)
  Sample rate: 44100 Hz
  Channels:   Mono or Stereo
  Duration:   0.5 – 2.0 seconds
  Volume:     Normalized to -3 dBFS peak

NOTE: Missing sound files cause silent notifications.
The mod will print a warning per missing file to the game log.
